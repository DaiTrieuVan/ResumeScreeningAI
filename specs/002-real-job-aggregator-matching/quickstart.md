# Quickstart: Real Job Aggregator & AI Matcher

## Validation Scenario
1. Navigate to Candidate Real Jobs Portal (`http://localhost:5173/real-jobs`).
2. Upload candidate CV PDF or select existing CV.
3. Trigger "Tìm Việc Thật Phù Hợp Với CV" (Scan Real Jobs).
4. Verify returned jobs display Match Score %, TopCV/ITViec badges, location, salary, and direct "Ứng tuyển ngay" apply links.
5. Filter by "Điểm khớp ≥ 80%" and "Hà Nội" to verify instant filter responsiveness (< 300ms).
