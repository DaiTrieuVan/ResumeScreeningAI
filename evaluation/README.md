# Evaluation datasets

Only synthetic or explicitly anonymized CV/JD fixtures may be stored here. Every published dataset must include a versioned manifest, provenance and license statement, label schema version, checksums, at least three job descriptions, and at least thirty candidate samples.

Raw personal CVs, contact details copied from real people, secrets, model caches, and generated reports must never be committed. Generated benchmark reports belong in `artifacts/benchmark/` and must contain aggregate measurements only.
